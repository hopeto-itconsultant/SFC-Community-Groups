13 October 2026

App launch

SFC Community Groups is live for leaders and admins. Sign in with the mobile number and password your Admin gave you.

  • Home — your group at a glance, this month’s numbers, and quick links to file a fellowship, follow-up, or plan the next meeting.
  • Reports — fellowships, follow-ups, next fellowship, and spending. Open a list or the calendar to find a day.
  • My Group — group photo, name, leaders, and edit the group profile.
  • Settings — who you are, change password, and sign out.
